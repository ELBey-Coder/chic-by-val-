Our story

Clothing chosen with care, not chased trends.

Chic by Val started with a simple idea: build a wardrobe of pieces you actually reach for — good fabric, a fit that lasts, and nothing you'll want to give away after one season.

Every item in this shop is selected personally, in small batches, so quality never gets diluted for the sake of volume. We'd rather offer twenty pieces we believe in than two hundred we don't.
