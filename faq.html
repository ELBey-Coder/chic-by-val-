Customer care

Questions, answered.

Everything to know before choosing your next Chic by Val piece.

How do I choose a size?

Each product page lists available sizes and materials. Contact Valerie with the product name if you want personal fit guidance.

Do you offer shipping or local pickup?

Complimentary local pickup is available in Indian Head, Maryland. Shipping availability and pricing appear at Stripe checkout.

What is the exchange policy?

Eligible unworn items with original tags may be exchanged within 14 days. Contact Valerie before sending anything back.

Is checkout secure?

Yes. Checkout is completed on Stripe’s secure website. Chic by Val does not see or store your full card information.

How can I contact Valerie?

Email achuval@yahoo.com or use the Contact page.
